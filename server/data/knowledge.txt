SUNRISE PUBLIC SCHOOL - TEST KNOWLEDGE BASE

SCHOOL INFORMATION
Sunrise Public School is a co-educational school located in Jaipur, Rajasthan. The school provides education from Class 1 to Class 12. The school focuses on academics, sports, technology, arts, and overall student development.

SCHOOL TIMINGS
The regular school day runs from 8:00 AM to 2:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Saturday is a half-day from 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM. The school remains closed on Sundays and officially declared holidays.

ADMISSIONS
Admissions are available for Classes 1 to 11 depending on seat availability. Parents must submit the student's birth certificate, previous school report card, transfer certificate when applicable, passport-size photographs, and parent or guardian identification. Admission to Classes 9 and 11 may require an entrance test.

FEES
The annual tuition fee for Classes 1 to 5 is ₹45,000. The annual tuition fee for Classes 6 to 8 is ₹55,000. The annual tuition fee for Classes 9 to 10 is ₹65,000. The annual tuition fee for Classes 11 to 12 is ₹75,000. Transportation and optional activity fees are charged separately.

ATTENDANCE
Students are expected to maintain at least 75% attendance during the academic year. Students with attendance below the required percentage may not be permitted to appear for final examinations unless an approved exception is granted by the school administration.

EXAMINATIONS
The school conducts two term examinations and regular periodic tests during the academic year. The first term examination is generally held in September and the final examination is generally held in March. The exact examination schedule is announced by the school administration.

HOLIDAYS
The school observes national holidays and a winter break in December. The annual holiday calendar is published at the beginning of each academic session. Parents should check official school notices for changes to holiday dates.

TEACHERS
The principal of Sunrise Public School is Dr. Ananya Sharma. The mathematics teacher for senior classes is Mr. Rahul Verma. The science teacher for Classes 9 and 10 is Ms. Priya Mehta. The English teacher for senior classes is Mrs. Neha Kapoor. The computer science teacher is Mr. Arjun Singh.

TRANSPORT
School bus transportation is available for students living in selected areas of Jaipur. Transport fees depend on the route. Parents must register for transportation with the school office before the beginning of the academic session.

LIBRARY
The school library is open from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM on school working days. Students from Class 1 to Class 12 can use the library. Students may borrow up to two books at a time for a period of fourteen days.

SPORTS
The school provides facilities for cricket, football, basketball, badminton, athletics, and table tennis. Students can participate in inter-school competitions after selection through school sports trials.

COMPUTER LAB
The school has a computer laboratory with internet-enabled computers. Computer lab sessions are included in the curriculum for students from Class 3 onward. Students must follow the school's computer and internet usage rules.

SCHOLARSHIPS
Merit-based scholarships are available for students who achieve outstanding academic results. Sports scholarships may also be offered to students who demonstrate exceptional performance at district or state level. Scholarship availability is subject to school rules and annual review.

CAFETERIA
The school cafeteria operates during school hours and provides snacks, beverages, and lunch items. Students are encouraged to choose healthy food options. Outside food should not be shared with other students due to allergy and safety concerns.

PARENT SUPPORT
Parents can contact the school office from Monday to Friday between 9:00 AM and 4:00 PM. For academic questions, parents may contact the relevant class teacher. Emergency matters should be reported directly to the school office.

DISCIPLINE
Students are expected to follow the school uniform, attendance, punctuality, and behavior rules. Bullying, harassment, vandalism, and cheating are not permitted. Disciplinary action may be taken when school rules are violated.

UNIFORM
The regular school uniform consists of a white shirt, navy-blue trousers or skirt, black shoes, navy-blue socks, and the school tie. Students must wear the prescribed sports uniform on designated sports days.

IDENTITY CARDS
Every student must carry the school identity card while on campus. Students may be asked to show their identity card when entering school facilities or participating in school activities.

EMERGENCY CONTACT
For urgent school-related matters, parents should contact the school office directly during working hours. The test school's office phone number is 0141-400-1234.

GENERAL FAQ
Students can access their academic notices through the school's student portal. Parents can use the portal to view attendance records, examination notices, and selected school announcements. Password-related problems should be reported to the school office.

The school does not guarantee admission solely on the basis of submitting an application. Admission depends on eligibility, available seats, and completion of the required admission process.

The school provides academic support sessions for students who need additional help in selected subjects. Students should contact their class teacher to request academic support.

The school organizes an annual science exhibition, sports day, cultural festival, and inter-school competition activities.
